Theme: Tracking the World of Work

This trimester connects English with careers, communication, interviews, skills, and your future professional path.

Main Question

How can English help you prepare for the world of work?

  • We will talk about careers and professional skills.
  • We will practice useful vocabulary for real-life situations.
  • We will prepare for interviews, presentations, and future opportunities.

Class Routine

How Classes Will Work

  • Warm-up questions and vocabulary review.
  • Reading, listening, or slide-based input.
  • Pair work and group interaction.
  • Speaking practice using sentence frames.
  • Short tasks on Inglês Paraná High.
  • Exit tickets, review, and feedback.
Inglês Paraná High

Platform Use

We will use Inglês Paraná High as part of the learning process.

  • Complete assigned activities.
  • Use the platform for practice and review.
  • Follow the digital learning trail.
  • Your participation may be monitored as part of your learning progress.

Project

My Professional Pathway

This trimester project will help you connect English with your future.

  • You will think about your skills and interests.
  • You will prepare language for professional situations.
  • You may create a professional profile, presentation, or interview simulation.
  • You will use English to present part of your professional identity.
Final Presentation

Culminância

At the end of the trimester, students may present their project through:

  • Mock Job Interview
  • Career Presentation
  • Professional Profile
  • Digital Presentation
  • Career Portfolio
  • Feira de Profissões em Língua Inglesa
Class Expectations

What I Expect From You

  • Participate, even if your English is not perfect.
  • Respect your classmates during speaking activities.
  • Complete classroom and platform tasks.
  • Use English as much as possible.
  • Ask for help when you need it.
  • Try, practice, improve.
Mindset
English is a tool.

You do not need perfect English to communicate. You need practice, courage, and consistency.
